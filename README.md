## 🔥 Why This Exists

AION 2 is free-to-play and built around Kinah. You need it for gear upgrades, crafting materials, potions, and even inventory slots. At level 20, the soft cap alone demands hundreds of thousands.

But NCSoft doesn't provide an auction house API. Players track prices in spreadsheets, notes apps, or memory. Meanwhile, open-world farming has a **daily cap of 1,000,000 Kinah per character**, and crafting profits depend on material costs vs. sale prices minus fees (10% server, 20% world).

This toolkit doesn't automate anything. It gives you the calculators and timers to make better decisions yourself.

## ❓ FAQ

**Does this connect to the game or auction house?**
No — NCSoft doesn't provide an auction API. This is a manual tracker. You input data, the toolkit calculates and organizes it.

**Will I get banned?**
No — this is a standalone notes and calculator app. It doesn't read game memory, inject code, or interact with the client. It's like using a spreadsheet.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes — the tracker is fully offline. All data is stored locally.

**Can I share data between characters?**
The tracker is designed for one character per profile. Multi-character support is on the roadmap.

**How accurate is the crafting calculator?**
It uses the fees you input (default: 10% server, 20% world) and your material costs. Accuracy depends on your inputs.

**How do I uninstall?**
Delete the toolkit folder. No registry entries or system changes are made.
